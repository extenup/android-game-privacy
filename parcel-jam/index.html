Extenup · Parcel Jam

Privacy policy

Effective 23 August 2026

Summary

The Android version of Parcel Jam does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Data stored on the device

The game stores unlocked levels, the current level, and best move counts in app-local storage. This information stays on the device and can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Accounts, advertising, and analytics

The Android application has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, purchases, social features, or cross-app tracking.

Permissions and network use

The application requests no sensitive permissions and does not access precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone. It can work offline after installation.

Children

Parcel Jam is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children. Extenup does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
