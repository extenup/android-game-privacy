Extenup · Emberless

Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Emberless is a wordless puzzle-platformer game. This Privacy Policy explains the information the Android app handles and how it is used.

Information the game keeps

To let you continue where you stopped, Emberless saves your game progress (such as the last chapter reached) and simple settings (such as whether sound is muted) in the app's private local storage on your device. You do not enter any personal information to play.

Data collection and sharing

Emberless does not collect, transmit, sell, or disclose personal information, device identifiers, gameplay data, analytics, diagnostics, or advertising data to Extenup or to third parties. The game runs entirely on your device.

The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, crash reporting, in-app purchases, or cloud processing, and it does not request the Internet permission.

Data retention and deletion

Saved progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, which removes them. Extenup cannot view or delete this data because Extenup never receives it.

Permissions

Emberless does not request Internet, storage, photos, camera, microphone, location, contacts, notifications, advertising ID, or other sensitive permissions.

Children's privacy

Emberless is not directed to children under 13. Extenup does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users through the game, because the game collects no personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, if advertising is added in a future version — this Privacy Policy will be updated at the same public location with a revised effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Emberless is published by Extenup. Privacy or support questions can be sent to extenup@gmail.com.
