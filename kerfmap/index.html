Extenup · KerfMap

Privacy policy

Effective 2 September 2026

KerfMap: Cut List Optimizer ("KerfMap") is an offline 1D cut-planning application. This Privacy Policy explains the information the app handles and how it is used.

Information you enter

KerfMap handles project information that you choose to enter, including project and material names, stock and part labels, lengths, quantities, kerf, end trim, reusable-offcut settings, and optional project notes.

This information is used only to save local projects, calculate and independently validate cut plans, display the results you request, and create a PDF when you choose that action.

Local processing and storage

KerfMap processes project information entirely on your device. Projects and project settings are kept in the app's private local storage. They are not uploaded to Extenup or a developer-operated server.

KerfMap does not provide cloud backup or synchronization. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the app's private data.

Data collection and sharing

KerfMap does not automatically collect, transmit, sell, or disclose personal information, project content, device identifiers, analytics, diagnostics, advertising data, or usage events to Extenup or third parties. The only outbound transfer is a PDF that you deliberately save or share through Android's system interface; KerfMap never sends that file to Extenup.

The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, cloud processing, or Internet permission. An optional link to this policy opens in a separate browser; KerfMap does not send project information with that link.

PDF files you choose to save or share

When you tap Save PDF, KerfMap asks the system document picker where to create the file. When you tap Share PDF, KerfMap creates a temporary PDF in its private cache and opens the system share sheet. Both actions begin only after you choose them.

A PDF can contain the project and material names, plan status, kerf and trim assumptions, stock and part labels and dimensions, cut layout, unplaced parts, optional project notes, and the safety reminder.

KerfMap does not scan your storage and does not request broad access to files. A saved PDF remains at the destination you selected until you delete it. For sharing, you select the receiving app, service, and recipient. They handle the shared copy under their own terms and privacy practices.

KerfMap removes older temporary share files when it prepares a new share copy. The current temporary copy can remain in private cache until a later share, until you clear the app's cache or storage, or until Android removes cached files.

Data retention and deletion

Local projects remain on your device until you delete them in KerfMap, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the app's private project data. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for this data.

PDF files saved outside KerfMap's private storage, and copies retained by a receiving app or service, are not deleted when you delete a project or uninstall KerfMap. Delete those copies from the location, app, or service where you chose to save or send them.

Extenup cannot view or delete local KerfMap projects or user-created PDF files because Extenup does not receive them.

Permissions and system access

KerfMap does not request Internet, broad storage, photos, camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, notifications, advertising ID, or other sensitive personal-data permissions. User-requested PDF operations use the system document picker, a restricted app FileProvider, and the system share sheet.

Children's privacy

KerfMap is a workshop-planning utility intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and Extenup does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users through KerfMap.

Safety

KerfMap is a planning aid, not a measuring device, fabrication controller, structural calculator, or safety system. Verify measurements, kerf, stock, tool setup, and cut sequence before cutting, and follow the instructions and safety procedures for your materials and tools.

Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same public location with a revised effective date.

Contact

KerfMap is published by Extenup. Privacy or support questions can be sent to extenup@gmail.com.
