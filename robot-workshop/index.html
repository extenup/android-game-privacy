Privacy policy

Kind Robot Workshop

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Kind Robot Workshop is an offline children's game published by Extenup. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information or sensitive data.

Data and permissions

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, social features, purchases or third-party network SDKs. It does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or an advertising identifier. The app does not use an internet permission.

Local storage

Completed levels and sound settings are stored only in the app's private storage on the device. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for this data. A parent can remove the saved progress from the parent section, and uninstalling the app also removes it.

Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact extenup@gmail.com.
